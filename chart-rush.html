Chart Rush

Two phones. One song. Whoever shouts the title first takes the board

Each round names an artist and hides their most-played songs behind a survey board. Guess what everyone else streams the most. Every round opens with a clip and a shared countdown — the first correct shout takes control.

What's in it

  • Face-off, survey board, three strikes and one steal, then Fast Money.
  • Practice solo on a single phone, with a streak and per-artist bests.
  • Host a night and share a code, or match with whoever else is waiting.
  • Connect Spotify — free — to build boards from music you actually listen to.
  • Host unlock ($4.99, one payment) adds Top 10 boards and five-survey nights. Friends always join free.

About Spotify

Connecting Spotify is optional and free. Chart Rush is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Spotify AB.
