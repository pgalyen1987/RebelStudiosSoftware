Event Tickets & Registration, with QR Check-in

Publish an event, let people register, and email each one a PDF ticket with a QR code. At the door, scan the QR with any phone to check them in — no separate app, no monthly fee.

The problem

Event ticketing on WordPress is dominated by a few big plugins that gate the basics behind expensive add-ons and bury simple RSVP-and-check-in under calendars you don’t need. The most-installed free option carries a wall of one- and two-star reviews — people just want to take a registration and scan a ticket at the door, and end up paying for three extensions to do it.

What it does

QR PDF tickets

Each attendee gets a PDF ticket with a unique QR code, emailed automatically on registration.

Scan to check in

A logged-in staff member scans the QR with a phone to mark the attendee present. The public only ever sees “valid ticket”.

Capacity handling

Set a capacity and the form shows seats-left and closes automatically when full.

Attendee list + CSV

See who’s registered and who’s checked in, and export the whole list to CSV.

Shortcodes

Drop [rs_events] for an upcoming list, or the registration form onto any event page.

Yours, private

Tickets and QR codes are generated on your server. Nothing phones home.
