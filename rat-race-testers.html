What you would be testing

You carry the only torch, which sounds like an advantage until you notice it lights about two metres of a maze you have never seen.

The other rats are out there somewhere. You find out they are close when their lantern light starts coming through the walls.

Every rat can sprint — about a second of speed, then five seconds of wishing you had saved it. Spend it on a straight and you take a corner off someone. Spend it just before a dead end and you have simply spent it.

Bring more rats and the map changes shape to match: five rats, five corners, a pentagon. Every corner sits the same distance from the cheese along the same shape of route, so nobody gets the short side and a loss is just a loss.

How testing works

  1. Open the opt-in link on your Android phone Signed in to the Google account you use on that phone. If Play says you need to be added first, email the address at the bottom and we will add you same day.
  2. Tap “Become a tester”, then install from Play It installs like any other app. Free.
  3. Leave it installed for fourteen days This is the part that actually counts. Google measures opt-in, not playtime — but leaving the programme resets the clock, so the ask is simply: don't uninstall.
  4. Tell us if anything is broken Entirely optional, and more useful than the fourteen days if you do.

Testing something yourself?

We will do the same for you. Send your opt-in link and we will install it, opt in, and stay in for the fourteen days. We have done this for Thymora, Quote & Invoice Maker PDF and Stamina.EXE, and we would rather trade than ask.
