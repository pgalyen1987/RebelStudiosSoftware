Restaurant menu & reservations

A throwaway WordPress with WooCommerce and the plugin already installed. It runs in your browser. Nothing is saved when you close the tab.

First boot is slow, about half a minute. You land already signed in as admin. The password is the WordPress Playground default.

  1. You land on Reservations. There is one pending booking for Jamie Rivera.
  2. Restaurant in the left menu is the menu items. Pages has Menu and Reservations on the front.
  3. The zip you buy adds online ordering on top of this.

This boots the free plugin. Playground is temporary; it is not a hosted restaurant site.
