Live demo

PDF invoices, packing slips, delivery notes

A throwaway WordPress with WooCommerce and the plugin already installed. It runs in your browser. Nothing is saved when you close the tab.

First boot is slow, about half a minute. You land already signed in as admin. The password is the WordPress Playground default.

  1. Orders is the first screen. Open the processing order for the Demo hoodie.
  2. The Invoices box is on the right. Print invoice, packing slip, or delivery note.
  3. WooCommerce → Invoices is the settings screen (logo, numbering, which email carries the PDF).

This boots the free plugin from WordPress.org. The zip you buy has Pro unlocked on top of it (bulk print from the orders list). Playground is temporary; it is not a hosted store.
