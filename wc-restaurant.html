Restaurant Menu, Reservations & QR Menu

Everything a restaurant needs on its site: a polished menu, table reservations with email confirmation, and a printable QR code that opens your menu — without a monthly SaaS bill.

The problem

Restaurants get pushed into $50–200/month platforms for things WordPress can do natively: show a menu, take a reservation, and put a QR code on the table. The existing free plugins are mostly abandoned — the popular restaurant-ordering plugin hasn’t shipped an update in over a year — so owners either pay a SaaS or run a menu that’s a PDF from 2019.

What it does

A menu that looks good

Dishes with price, description, photo and dietary tags, grouped into sections. Drop it anywhere with [rs_menu].

Table reservations

A clean booking form. The guest gets a confirmation email; you get a notification and confirm or cancel from the admin — which emails the guest.

Printable QR menu

A QR code that opens your menu page, ready to print for your tables. Generated on your server.

No monthly fee

This replaces the part of a restaurant SaaS most owners actually use — without the subscription.

Yours to own

Your menu and reservations live in your WordPress, not a third-party platform that can raise prices.

Ready for ordering

When you’re ready, Pro adds online ordering for pickup and delivery on the same menu.

Pricing

The plugin is free on WordPress.org. Pro adds the power features for teams and busy stores — one licence, a year of updates and support.
