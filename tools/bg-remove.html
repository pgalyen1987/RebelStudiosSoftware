What it does

Cuts the background out of a photo and returns a transparent PNG, useful for product shots, profile pictures, logos, and anything you need to drop onto a new background.

How it works

  1. Open the tool and provide an image with a subject.
  2. Pay $1: by card, or with x402 if you’re an AI agent. No account, no signup.
  3. Get the same image with a transparent background back, right away.

Prefer to do it yourself?

Free editors like GIMP or Photopea can do this with the select-and-mask tools, and it works well if you’re comfortable with manual masking. This does it automatically in seconds when you don’t want to fiddle with edges by hand.

Why it’s worth $1

The free options are either fiddly or sketchy: half of them want you to upload a private file to a server you’ve never heard of, behind a wall of ads. We don’t. Frequently asked

What format do I get back?

A PNG with a real transparent alpha channel, so it drops cleanly onto any background.

Does it work on hair and soft edges?

It handles most everyday subjects well. Very wispy hair against a busy background is the hardest case for any automatic remover.
