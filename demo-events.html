Event tickets

A throwaway WordPress with WooCommerce and the plugin already installed. It runs in your browser. Nothing is saved when you close the tab.

First boot is slow, about half a minute. You land already signed in as admin. The password is the WordPress Playground default.

  1. You land on Events. Open Saturday matinee.
  2. View the event on the front of the site. Register, then check the attendee list.
  3. The zip you buy adds ticket types and the door-scan QR on top of this.

This boots the free plugin. Playground is temporary; it is not a hosted box office. There is also a 49-second screen recording on the product page if you want the door-scan path without waiting for boot.
