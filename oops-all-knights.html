Chess variant · free

Oops All Knights

Each side gets a king and eight knights. No pawns, no bishops, no rooks, no queen. Every piece jumps, so there are no lines to hold, nothing can be pinned, and the threat comes from the square you weren’t watching.

Play in your browser

The browser version needs no install and no account. Android is coming to Google Play; it’s in closed testing now.

What changes when every piece jumps

Nothing can be pinned.
A knight’s attack can’t be blocked, so moving one of your knights never uncovers an attack on your king. Every knight is free to move unless your king is already in check.
A check has two answers, not three.
Move the king, or take the knight giving check. There is nothing to put in the way.
No discovered attacks, no double check.
Both need a piece that attacks along a line, and there isn’t one on the board.
Only the king can lose a tempo.
A knight lands on the other colour every move, so it needs an even number of moves to get back to any square. When an endgame comes down to who has to move, the king is the only piece that can pass the move back.

The rules

Setup
White’s knights stand on a2 to h2, Black’s on a7 to h7. The kings start on e1 and e8, alone on their back ranks. White moves first.
Moving
Knights move in an L, jumping over anything in the way, and capture by landing on an enemy piece. Kings step one square in any direction and may never move into check.
Winning
Checkmate.
Draws
Stalemate. A board with one knight or none left on it, since a lone knight can’t mate. Fifty moves by each side without a capture: with no pawns, a capture is the only move that can’t be undone, so this is what stops two knights circling forever.
Not in this game
Castling, promotion and en passant. There are no rooks or pawns to make them possible.

Two ways to play

On one screen

Pass and play: two people, one phone or one browser tab. You can take a move back.

Online

Create a room and you get a six-character code; your friend types it under Join a room. Find an opponent puts you in a queue with whoever else is looking for a game right then. The queue is only as busy as the people in it, so a room code is the sure way to get a game.

What it doesn’t have

No clock or time controls. No ratings, no accounts, no move list and no PGN export. If you need those for serious play, they aren’t here yet. The iPhone app is free and shows ads; neither version asks who you are.
