The build is easy. Signing is the hard part.

An iOS distribution certificate made on Linux, ready for a macOS CI runner.

bash · no Mac involved
# 1. your private key, and a signing request for developer.apple.com
$ openssl genrsa -out ios_distribution.key 2048
$ openssl req -new -key ios_distribution.key -out CertificateSigningRequest.certSigningRequest
# 2. Apple's certificate back, to PEM, joined with the key
$ openssl x509 -inform DER -in distribution.cer -out distribution.pem
$ openssl pkcs12 -export -inkey ios_distribution.key -in distribution.pem -out ios_distribution.p12
# 3. into a CI secret
$ base64 -w0 ios_distribution.p12 > p12.b64
