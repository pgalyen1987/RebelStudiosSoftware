Before you interact with a token contract

Six checks, and the trap each one catches.

1Sell simulationA honeypot lets you buy, then blocks every sell but the deployer's.
2Buy and sell taxA 40% sell tax, or one the owner can raise later, is a slow rug.
3Liquidity depth and lockIf the LP tokens aren't locked, the deployer can pull the pool.
4Holder concentrationOne or two wallets holding most of the supply can dump on you.
5Contract permissionsMint, pause, blacklist or raise the tax: what the owner kept.
6Basic ERC-20 sanityVerified source, not a look-alike with a modified transfer.
