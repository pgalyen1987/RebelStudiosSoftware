Invoices, Packing Slips & Delivery Notes for WooCommerce

Give every order a clean PDF invoice, packing slip and delivery note — generated on your own server, attached to the right order email automatically, and printable in one click. Built for the current WooCommerce: block checkout and HPOS.

The problem

The popular invoice plugins for WooCommerce share the same three complaints in their one-star reviews: the output looks dated and can’t be customised, non-Latin and right-to-left languages render as broken boxes, and the things a store actually needs — like tax lines — are locked behind the paid version. On top of that, several were written before the block checkout and HPOS, so they quietly break on a modern store.

What it does

Real PDFs, on your server

Generated with a bundled engine — no third-party service, nothing phones home. Your logo is embedded, so rendering makes zero external requests.

Attached to the right email

Attach the invoice PDF to the Processing or Completed order email automatically. One setting.

Every language

Right-to-left and accented/non-Latin scripts render correctly — the exact thing that’s broken elsewhere.

Stable numbering

Sequential or order-number, with a prefix. The number is stored on the order, so a reprint never changes.

Never breaks the store

No PDF engine on the host? Documents fall back to a clean printable page — the order email still sends.

One-click from the order

Print or download an invoice, packing slip or delivery note straight from the order screen (classic and HPOS).
