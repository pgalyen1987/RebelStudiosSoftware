Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 23, 2026

Rebel Studios Software (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates rebelstudiossoftware.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information:

  • Contact form data: If you email us through our contact form, we receive your name, email address, and message content.
  • Automatically collected data: Like most websites, our hosting provider and third-party services may collect standard log data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URLs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: Our blog uses cookies for advertising purposes through Google AdSense (see below).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and project requests
  • Improve our website and content
  • Serve relevant advertisements on our blog
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Google AdSense

Our blog displays advertisements served by Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC. Google and its partners may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

When you submit our contact form, your message is processed by FormSubmit.co to deliver it to our email inbox. FormSubmit may temporarily process your name, email, and message content for delivery purposes.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies and How to Control Them

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device. On our blog, cookies are used primarily by Google AdSense and its advertising partners to measure ad performance and, where permitted, to personalize the ads you see. Third parties, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and most browsers let you block third-party cookies entirely. Disabling cookies will not break the Site, though some advertisements may become less relevant. You can also manage ad personalization directly through the Google and industry opt-out links in the section above.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns — which pages are popular, how visitors arrive, and general device and country information. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

Third-Party Links and Services

Our website links to external sites and relies on a small number of third-party services, including GitHub, Google Play, our product sites (trade-guard.pro and gate402.app), Google AdSense for advertising, and FormSubmit.co for contact-form delivery. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party websites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Data Retention

Contact inquiries are retained only as long as needed to respond and manage our business relationship. Server logs and advertising data are retained according to our hosting and advertising providers’ policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we handle, including serving the entire Site over encrypted HTTPS. No method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data. Under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), these can include the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, and to object to certain processing. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion, and to opt out of any “sale” of personal information — we do not sell personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below and we will respond as required by applicable law.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@rebelstudiossoftware.com.
