About Rebel Studios

We build apps, ship SaaS, and publish research, for clients and for ourselves.

Who We Are

Rebel Studios Software is an independent software studio founded by Patrick Galyen. We build full-stack web and SaaS products, WordPress and WooCommerce plugins, browser and editor extensions, mobile apps, and onchain apps on Base and Farcaster, with a focus on shipping real products, not slide decks.

Every project in our portfolio is something we built and shipped: apps live on Google Play, SaaS platforms in production, plugins and extensions people install today, and open-source research like Vigilo for on-device network anomaly detection.

How We Work

We are a small studio, which means the person who scopes your project is the person who builds it. There is no account manager relaying requirements to an offshore team you never meet. That keeps communication direct and the work honest: we would rather show you something running than describe something on a slide.

Scope & Plan

We start by understanding the actual problem: who it is for, what “done” looks like, and what the real constraints are. Then we agree on a concrete plan and price before writing code.

Build in the Open

You see working software early and often, not a big reveal at the end. Shipping a rough version that works beats polishing something that never launches. We have written about why.

Ship & Support

We handle the unglamorous last mile: store submission, deployment, monitoring, and the fixes that follow. Every product in our portfolio is one we took all the way to real users.

Selected Work

A few things we have designed, built, and shipped end to end: TradeGuard Pro, a production SaaS for construction subcontractor compliance; Gate402, an x402 payment gateway that lets AI agents pay for APIs per call; Phuzzles, a photo-puzzle messaging app on Google Play; and Vigilo, open-source on-device IoT anomaly detection. Browse the full website and app portfolios, or read the blog where we write up how the hard parts actually work.

Patrick Galyen

Patrick is a software engineer with about ten years of experience building reliable, performance-critical systems. At Booz Allen Hamilton he was the primary developer of a multithreaded C++ state machine coordinating UDP messaging across networks, built CI/CD pipelines deploying to AWS, and improved application performance by 33% through benchmarking and refactoring. Before that he built C# and C++ applications at Vectrus and C++11 systems on Red Hat Linux at AECOM, and afterwards hardened build infrastructure and Jenkins pipelines as a DevSecOps engineer at Precise Systems.

Since 2025 he has run Rebel Studios full time and built everything in this portfolio end to end: TradeGuard, a construction-compliance SaaS with Stripe billing, QuickBooks integration and automated certificate-of-insurance extraction; gate402, a pay-per-call API gateway for AI agents with published npm and PyPI packages; the WooCommerce plugins and browser extensions sold here; Farcaster mini apps on Base; and open-source security research including Vigilo.

He works across Python, C++, C#, TypeScript and Solidity (Next.js, Prisma, Stripe, Foundry, Docker, AWS and CI/CD), holds a B.S. in Computer Science with a minor in Data Science, and is an ICAgile Certified Professional. He is available for contract and consulting work.
