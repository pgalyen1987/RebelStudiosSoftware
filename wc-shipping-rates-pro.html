The part nobody else gives you

Every table-rate plugin lets you build a table. The problem with a table is the day it returns $14.95 and you cannot work out which of your eleven rows did it, because the rows overlap and the one you expected lost on a bound you forgot you set.

The cart simulator answers that question directly. Give it product IDs and a destination and it tells you which rule fired, and for each row that did not, the specific condition that excluded it — under the minimum weight, over the subtotal cap, wrong zone, wrong shipping class. It turns a guessing game into a readout.

What Pro adds

This is the complete list. The free plugin below does the table; these are the things on top of it.

Shipping-class conditions

Put a condition on a rate row so it only applies to products you have tagged — fragile, oversized, frozen, whatever your classes are.

Stop after match

Once a row hits, later rows are not evaluated. This is how you express priority instead of hoping the ordering works out.

Only this

When a rate matches, hide the other shipping methods on that package — so an oversized item cannot be checked out as a letter.

CSV import and export

Edit the table in a spreadsheet and import it back. Not a separate paid add-on, which is how this is usually sold.

Cart simulator

Which rule matched, and why each other row failed. Described above, because it is the reason to buy this.

What the free plugin does

Pro is an add-on. The table itself, and everything needed to charge for shipping, is in the free plugin:

  • Table rates per shipping zone — a flat cost, with optional per-item and per-weight add-ons.
  • Conditions — minimum and maximum weight, cart subtotal and quantity. Leave a bound blank to skip it.
  • Your own labels — “Ground”, “2-day”, “Free over $75”; whatever you actually charge.
  • HPOS and block checkout — declared compatible with current WooCommerce.
  • No Jetpack, no carrier login, no monthly fee — the rate tables live in your own WordPress options.

Your rate tables are stored on your site and the plugin makes no external requests. Nothing about your orders leaves your server to decide a shipping price.

What it does not do

Worth stating plainly, so you can rule it out in thirty seconds rather than after paying:

  • It does not buy postage labels. It replaces the checkout rate decision with rates you set. If you need a printable label, use your carrier’s site or a label tool after the order is paid.
  • It does not fetch live carrier rates. There is no UPS, FedEx or USPS account to connect — that is the trade for having no monthly fee and no external requests, and if live rates are what you need, this is the wrong product.
  • It does not do box packing or dimensional weight. Conditions are weight, subtotal, quantity and shipping class.
  • The free plugin is not on WordPress.org yet. It is a GPLv3 plugin and both zips come with your purchase, so a buyer does not need the directory — but if you were expecting to install the free half from there first, you cannot today.
