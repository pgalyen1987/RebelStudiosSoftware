Membership & content restriction

A throwaway WordPress with the plugin already installed. It runs in your browser. Nothing is saved when you close the tab.

First boot is slow, about half a minute. You land already signed in as admin. The password is the WordPress Playground default.

  1. You land on Membership in wp-admin. That is the settings screen.
  2. Posts has “Members only note.” View it, then log out (or open it in a private window) to see the gate.
  3. Pages has Become a member and Log in, which are the front-end shortcodes.

This boots the free plugin. The zip you buy has Pro unlocked on top of it (paid plans through WooCommerce). Playground is temporary; it is not a hosted members site.
