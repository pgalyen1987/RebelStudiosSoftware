Table-rate shipping you control

A throwaway WordPress with WooCommerce and the free plugin already installed. It runs in your browser. Nothing is saved when you close the tab.

First boot is slow, about half a minute. You land already signed in as admin. The password is the WordPress Playground default. WooCommerce and the free plugin install from our hosted zip — no WordPress.org wait.

  1. Skip the WooCommerce setup wizard if it appears.
  2. WooCommerce → Settings → Shipping → open a zone → Add shipping method → Rebel Shipping Rates.
  3. Fill a couple of rows (e.g. free over a cart total; flat rate below that). Save. Add a product with a weight, go to the storefront cart, and confirm the label.

This Playground demo is the free plugin only. The Codester zip includes Pro unlocked (class conditions, CSV, rate simulator). Playground is temporary; it is not a hosted store.
